How it works

  1. Confirm the student name and grade (already filled in).
  2. Enter the amount and your M-Pesa phone number.
  3. Click Pay with M-Pesa — an M-Pesa prompt fires on your phone within seconds.

  5. We unlock your results portal automatically when the payment confirms.
